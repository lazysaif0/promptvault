Fill in the form and PromptVault builds a ready-to-send entry for you. Nothing is uploaded. Everything happens in your browser, so it works the same on GitHub Pages.

    your-entry.json

    Your entry is ready. Choose how to send it:

    1. Easiest: open a GitHub issue. Your prompt is pre-filled and a maintainer will add it.
    2. Pull request: copy the JSON, open prompts.json on GitHub, paste it at the end of the "prompts" list (add a comma before it), and commit. GitHub will offer to open a pull request.
    3. Keep a file: download the JSON and share it however you like.
